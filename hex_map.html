Import the Universe

⚠ EXPERIMENTAL FEATURE — USE AT YOUR OWN RISK ⚠
Import the Universe will:
• Resize the canvas to 16×8 sectors (128 positions)
• Erase ALL existing hex data and routes
• Fetch up to 128 sectors from travellermap.com
   (cached sectors load instantly; live fetches are 1 second apart)
• Then build a full Mongoose 2e system for every world, one sector at a time. The map stays usable, and Stop is on the card at the bottom right.
• Published routes load into their own slots. Trading Route stays empty.

This cannot be undone.
