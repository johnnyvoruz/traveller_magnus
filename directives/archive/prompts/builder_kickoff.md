Issued 2026-10-08 by the orchestrator. The character MVP is live and Johnny has looked:
"MVP looks good." His next direction, in his words:

> "We would probably do universe building stuff, so that we can click and generate / edit

campaign. **Read `plan.md` "Slice 2 — Builder", `architecture.md` sections 2, 4, 5 and 8,

## Agent B: the server, as it stands, against the Builder (read-only)

Write `findings/builder_server_audit.md`:
1. What a signed-in universe can do to a hex today, route by route
   (`routes/universes.ts`, `generate.ts`, `objects.ts`, the universe Durable Object):
   read, generate one hex, generate many, write an edited tree, clear, the override row
   and its `base_hash`, history and snapshots. For each: built, partly built, or only in
   the spec, with the file and function.
2. For **system CRUD first**: the exact calls a browser would make to create a system in
   an empty hex (generated by a chosen engine, or blank), to edit one (a tree edited in
   the browser, hashed on the server), to regenerate one, and to delete one back to the
   truth or to empty. What is missing for each, as a list small enough that each item is
   one step. Then the same, more briefly, for a subsector, a sector, and for routes,
   borders, regions and allegiances.
3. What "pinned to the truth" and "own map" mean in the code today, and whether a
   campaign universe (which holds no map edits by the plan) can become a builder universe
   or needs a new kind.
4. The cost: what a generated system writes (objects, rows), what a full sector does to
   the Durable Object and to the queue, and what in the parked truth-build hardening on
   disk (do not continue it) the Builder would need.
5. **Johnny wants to rebuild the planetary generator later** on a foundation he
   understands, and dislikes chart-wide passes on the server. Say where the Builder
   depends on the present engines' output shape, so the build steps can keep that
   dependency behind one seam.
6. **Separately, `findings/signin_setup.md`, for Johnny to follow himself.** The code
   knows Discord and Google (`apps/api/src/auth/options.ts`) and only X is switched on.
   The exact steps: what to create in each provider's console, the exact redirect
   addresses to enter for production and for local use (read them from the code, do not
   guess), the names of the Worker secrets and the exact `wrangler secret put` lines,
   what he will see when it works, and anything in the web sign-in card that must change
   to show the new buttons (say who would change it; do not edit it).

app's one panel system and the legacy look that is the bar:
1. **Selecting to build.** With a universe open: one hex, many hexes, an empty hex, a
