          Surgeon Tobias Hale Found Dead

          Investigators confirm the death of Dr. Tobias Hale, the physician credited with thousands of lives saved across the subsector. No suspect has been named. Security records from the scene were reported corrupted. Memorial services were held on eleven worlds.

          Everyone knew Tobias Hale. The surgeon who went where the autodocs did not, into collapsed mines and burning refugee holds, and came back out with the living. Some of you saw his face on the newsfeeds. Some of you may have seen it closer than that, leaning over you in a field hospital, telling you to stay awake.

          Then he was murdered. The killer was never found. There were rumors about the security footage, the kind of rumors people tell late at night in starport bars. Nobody took them seriously.

          Wreck of the Amuar

          The Amuar was supposed to be dead. No power. No crew. No survivors.

          One low berth was still running.

          The lid hissed open. The man inside was breathing, slow and steady. Then his eyes opened and he was on his feet before anyone could raise a weapon, scanning the room, measuring every one of you.

          His gaze stopped on a cut along someone's forearm. Before anyone spoke he was moving, hands already working, pressure, elevation, a field dressing pulled from a kit he should not have known was there. Clean, fast and certain.

          You know that face. It was on every newsfeed for a month.

          Tobias Hale is dead. You watched the memorial.

          When someone finally asks his name, he hesitates. Not long. Just long enough to notice.

          JAGO

          He does not know how he got here. He does not know what year it is. When you say the name Hale he looks at you with the honest confusion of a man hearing a stranger's name.

          But he knows medicine better than anyone you have ever met.

          And for reasons he cannot explain, he wants to come with you.
