          Dr. Tobias Hale was a legend. A surgeon who went where the autodocs did not, into collapsed mines and burning refugee holds, and came back out with the living. Across a dozen systems the grateful knew his name.

          What they did not know was what his name had been used to build.

          Project MERIDIAN existed on no ledger. Its money moved through shell corporations and research grants that led nowhere. In a facility that appeared on no survey, twelve men were grown from Hale's cells, forced through childhood in a handful of years and trained until surgery came to them as easily as breathing. They were built to work alone in the worst places in charted space. They were built to work as a unit, and together they moved like a single organism.

          And they were built with a limit. Something in the gene-work or the conditioning or both. A wall in the mind that rose the instant they tried to end a life. Men who could open a body in ninety seconds could never be allowed to become weapons.

          Then Tobias Hale was found dead in his quarters.

          The newsfeeds ran it for weeks. Beloved surgeon murdered. Killer never identified. The security footage was declared corrupted and sealed. The few who saw it before the seal said it showed Hale walking out of his own room an hour after he died.

          Within a month MERIDIAN was gone. Within two, so was every record of its subjects.

          Low berth cycle complete

          Cold.

          That came first. Cold and the hiss of a seal releasing and light stabbing through his eyelids. Then voices. Strangers. Armed and wary.

          Get up. Assess. Move.

          He was on his feet before he decided to stand. His eyes went to the nearest of them. A gash along the forearm, shallow, bleeding more than it should. Pressure. Elevate. Three sutures. He knew it the way he knew his own pulse.

          He did not know where he was. He did not know how long he had been in the berth or who had put him there. He reached for a past and found fog, and behind the fog a single word.

          Jago.

          One of them was staring at him. Staring at his face as though it belonged to a dead man. Why?

          Something else stirred. A pull toward these people that made no sense. He had known them for thirty seconds. Already he needed to help them, to stand with them, to be one of them.

          Later a flash would come. A firing range. A pistol in his hand and a target shaped like a man. His finger on the trigger. His finger refusing. His hand gone numb and his vision collapsing to grey, his own body betraying him an inch from the kill.

          Someone had made him this way.

          He would find out who.

          For the Referee

          Identity

          MERIDIAN produced twelve clones of Tobias Hale. Jago is MERIDIAN-03 and does not know his designation.

          Public Record

          The project was off the books. The public knows only that Hale was murdered.

          Subject 06

          Those who know about MERIDIAN suspect MERIDIAN-06 of the murder. His guilt is unresolved.

          The Others

          The whereabouts and loyalties of the remaining clones are unknown. Some may carry the same inhibitor. Some may not.

          Inhibitor

          Jago cannot knowingly kill or cause lethal harm. Attempting it brings numbness and greying vision. He fights with non-lethal weapons. He still chooses whom to treat.

          Team Drive

          Jago was built to operate in a unit. He bonds quickly with the party and moves to help them at once.

          Implants

          MERIDIAN fitted every clone with a Metastatic Platelet Injection and a Personal Automedic. The platelets leave pale blue scabs and scars, a tell that can mark the other clones too.

          Contacts

          Both Contacts know Jago. He does not remember either of them. Either can recognize him at the worst possible moment.

          Dr. Hale

          Hale may have known more about MERIDIAN than his reputation suggests.

          Referee's Discretion

          Everything above is a starting point. The cause of Jago's amnesia, how he came to be aboard the Amuar, who killed Hale and what the other clones are doing now are yours to decide. Change, expand or discard any of it to fit your campaign.
